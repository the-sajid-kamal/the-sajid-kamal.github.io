## What I did

- Built three brands from zero: Yum, Funtastic and Fast.
- Developed and launched 22 cake, 12 bread and 6 bun SKUs across the three brands, and oversaw development of new products (14 cakes, 7 breads and 3 chips).

- Delivered 17 activations (spot, road, school and residential), two 360° thematic campaigns, a 360° CSR campaign and 11 digital campaigns, including setting up every digital platform.
- Created content for three Facebook pages, three Instagram accounts and a LinkedIn page.
- Ran consumer preference research on brand elements (corporate logo, the mother brand name and logo, Bakeman’s, and four pack designs).
