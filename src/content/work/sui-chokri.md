## The idea

Sui Chokri was a distributed clothing production platform. It connected neighbourhood tailors to customers and indie brands through centrally managed hubs. I built it alongside my full-time job.

[Placeholder: the problem you saw for tailors or for small clothing brands, in 2–3 sentences.]

## What I did

- Designed a hub-based operating model that borrows FMCG distribution principles to coordinate orders, production capacity and quality across independent tailor shops.
- Onboarded 156 tailor shops in Mohammadpur, Mirpur and Rampura.
- Was selected for the Needle Innovation Challenge, under the Oporajita initiative of the H&M Foundation, supported by Sweden and The Asia Foundation, and received a pollination grant to build and pilot the model.
- Tested and pivoted the business model based on what the pilot showed.
