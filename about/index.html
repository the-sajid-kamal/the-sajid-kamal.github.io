About

Hello, I’m Sajid. I build brands people reach for.

I’m an FMCG brand strategist in Dhaka, with more than six years of experience in new product development, digital marketing and integrated campaigns.

Today I’m Brand Manager at Ghorer Bazar. I’ve built new categories there, from a honey portfolio to mustard oil, gawa ghee and functional foods, and I’m designing the company’s first route-to-market for retail.

Before that I worked at Akij Bakers, Meghna Group and Remark HB: building three bakery brands from scratch, designing trade programs that lifted noodle sales by 172%, launching the Herlan cosmetics portfolio and running more than 100 digital campaigns.

Alongside my job I founded Sui Chokri, an “Uber for tailor shops” that connected 156 neighbourhood tailors with customers and indie brands, supported by the H&M Foundation’s Needle Innovation Challenge.

I studied MIS and Marketing at North South University. Before marketing, I trained more than 700 volunteers in emergency first aid with Criticalink.

Sajid Kamal is an FMCG brand strategist based in Dhaka and Brand Manager at Ghorer Bazar. Over six years at Akij Bakers, Meghna Group, Remark HB and Ghorer Bazar, Sajid has built three brands from scratch, launched new food and beauty categories and led more than 100 digital campaigns. Sajid is also the founder of Sui Chokri, a distributed production network for neighbourhood tailors supported by the H&M Foundation’s Needle Innovation Challenge.
