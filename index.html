About me

{%- comment -%} COPY: your point of view in one sentence. Alternatives in CONTENT-GUIDE.md {%- endcomment %}
Great brands are built where people buy, not only where they scroll.

I started in brand management at Akij Bakers in 2020, building three bakery brands from nothing: Yum, Funtastic and Fast. Since then I’ve worked across food, health and beauty, designing trade programs at Meghna Group, launching Herlan cosmetics at Remark HB, running more than 100 digital campaigns and, today, building new categories at Ghorer Bazar.

Alongside my job I founded Sui Chokri, which connected 156 neighbourhood tailor shops with customers and indie brands.
